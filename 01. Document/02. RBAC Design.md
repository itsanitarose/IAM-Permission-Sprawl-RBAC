# 02. RBAC Design

## 1. Access Review by Employee

I compared each employee's assigned permissions with their job responsibilities to identify unnecessary access.

| Employee | Role | Access to Remove | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ana | Billing Clerk | ⚠️ Export all billing data | Not required for her role |
| Ben | Billing Clerk | ⚠️ Approve refunds, Create new payee | Exceeds Clerk responsibilities |
| Chloe | Billing Clerk | ✓ None | Access matches job requirements |
| Dev | Billing Supervisor | ⚠️ Create new payee, Approve payments, Export all billing data | Exceeds Supervisor responsibilities |
| Erin | Finance Manager | ⚠️ Approve refunds | Not required for her role |
| Fraser | IT Support | ⚠️ All six billing and financial permissions | IT Support should not perform financial operations |

**Result:** Five accounts require permission changes. Chloe's access already matches her job responsibilities.

Fraser's System Admin permission also requires review to ensure it is limited to authorised technical tasks.

## 2. Proposed RBAC Model

I designed four job-based roles to replace individually assigned permissions.

| Role | Assigned Permissions |
|---|---|
| Billing Clerk | Create invoices, Edit patient billing details |
| Billing Supervisor | Create invoices, Edit patient billing details, Approve refunds |
| Finance Manager | Approve payments, Export all billing data |
| IT Support | System admin, restricted to authorised technical tasks |

Each role would be managed through a dedicated security group.

### Proposed Security Groups

| Security Group | Role |
|---|---|
| `SG_Billing_Clerk` | Billing Clerk |
| `SG_Billing_Supervisor` | Billing Supervisor |
| `SG_Finance_Manager` | Finance Manager |
| `SG_IT_Support` | IT Support |

**Create new payee** is excluded from the proposed roles because none of the supplied job descriptions requires it. Finance would need to confirm ownership and approval requirements before assigning this permission.

## 3. Design Decisions

- **Least Privilege**  
  Each role receives only the permissions necessary for its documented responsibilities.

<br>

- **Segregation of Duties (SoD)**  
  Payee creation and payment approval should remain separate to reduce the risk of unauthorised transactions.  
  Billing Supervisor access also requires compensating controls because the role combines invoice creation with refund approval.

<br>

- **Privileged Access**  
  IT Support should be able to maintain and troubleshoot the system without permanent financial permissions.  
  Temporary administrative elevation through PIM/JIT is recommended where supported, with approval, time limits, and audit logging.

<br>

- **Business Validation**  
  The proposed roles and permissions must be confirmed with business owners before implementation.
