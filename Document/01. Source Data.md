## 2. Job Descriptions

This is what each role actually needs:

- **Billing Clerk:** Create invoices, update patient billing details.
- **Billing Supervisor:** Billing Clerk access, plus approve refunds.
- **Finance Manager:** Approve payments, export billing data for monthly reporting.
- **IT Support:** Keep the system running, with no involvement in financial decisions.
